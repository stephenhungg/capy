# capy kawaii brand lock

## product

capy is the experience network for physical intelligence. it turns robot failures into targeted experience requests, verifies capability improvement, and rewards the contributors responsible for that improvement.

## mascot

a small, round capybara field engineer with warm cocoa fur, a cream muzzle, blush cheeks, a mint utility vest, and a tiny orange telemetry badge. the capybara carries a capability-receipt tablet and may interact with a friendly robotic gripper. proportions are chibi, the silhouette is simple, and the expression is calm, capable, and quietly optimistic.
