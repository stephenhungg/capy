# standards and regulatory map

reviewed 2026-08-30. this map uses primary regulator, legislature, standards-body, and protocol sources. it is an engineering crosswalk, not a legal opinion. scope, exemptions, definitions, effective dates, conflicts of law, and contractual terms require qualified counsel in every production jurisdiction.

## regulatory anchors

| source | capy control | limit and counsel gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [eu general data protection regulation, regulation (eu) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) | purpose limitation, minimization, lawful-basis record, special-category review, transparency, data-subject requests, erasure/objection impact, privacy by design, processor/transfer records, breach decisions | applies only after role, territorial scope, data identifiability, lawful basis, article 9 condition, research rules, transfers, and exemptions are analyzed. withdrawal and erasure are not absolute. counsel required. |
| [edpb guidelines 05/2020 on consent](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-052020-consent-under-regulation-2016679_en) | affirmative, specific, informed, granular, freely given, demonstrable, and easy-to-withdraw consent | consent may be invalid under power imbalance or unnecessary bundling. another basis cannot be swapped in casually. counsel required for contributor/worker relationships. |
| [edpb opinion 28/2024 on data protection and AI models](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/opinion-of-the-board-art-64/opinion-282024-on-certain-data-protection-aspects-related-to_en) | case-specific assessment of model anonymity, extraction/identification likelihood, legitimate-interest analysis, and consequences of unlawfully processed development data | a model is not automatically anonymous. whether a trained model contains personal data and what remediation is required are technical/legal questions for privacy engineering and counsel. |
| [eu artificial intelligence act, regulation (eu) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) | lifecycle governance, data governance, technical documentation, logging, human oversight, risk management, transparency, and general-purpose model training-content/license records where applicable | role, system classification, prohibited/high-risk/GPAI scope, phased application dates, standards, and interaction with product-safety/data law require counsel. this design does not claim AI Act conformity. |
| [eu directive 2019/790 on copyright in the digital single market](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/790/oj) | license and rights-reservation fields; machine-readable text/data-mining reservation review | national transposition, lawful access, article 3/4 scope, reservation form, database rights, and non-eu law vary. a TDM exception is not consent or blanket model authorization. counsel required. |
| [california consumer privacy act and regulations, effective 2026](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/) | notice, purpose/use limitation, sensitive-data handling, request workflow, retention disclosure, risk assessment, cybersecurity and automated-decision records where in scope | business/consumer/employee scope, thresholds, exemptions, service-provider contracts, phased deadlines, and other california laws require counsel. schema support does not establish applicability or compliance. |
| [ftc policy statement on biometric information and section 5](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/policy-statement-federal-trade-commission-biometric-information-section-5-federal-trade-commission) | biometric-risk flag, substantiated claims, minimization, consent/notice evidence, security, bias testing, retention/destruction, and incident handling | a policy statement is not a safe harbor and state biometric, recording, publicity, employment, and consumer laws may be stricter. counsel required before biometric identification or inference. |
| [illinois biometric information privacy act, 740 ILCS 14](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=3004&ChapterID=57) | written notice/release evidence and biometric retention/destruction routing | definitions, exclusions, private-action exposure, and current amendments require illinois counsel. images or audio and derived geometry/templates can be treated differently. |
| [ftc COPPA final rule amendments](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/federal-register-notices/16-cfr-part-312-coppa-final-rule-amendments) | hard stop for possible under-13 collection, verified parental authority, separate disclosure choices, security, and purpose-bound retention | applicability and interaction with stricter state child/privacy laws require counsel. an ordinary contributor release cannot substitute for verified parental consent where COPPA applies. |

the jurisdiction record intentionally does not encode a universal list of law names or deadlines. recording consent, biometric statutes, publicity/personality rights, workplace monitoring, children/minors, tax, sanctions, export control, payment services, labor classification, sector privacy, and breach notice are highly location- and fact-specific. production routing rules must come from a counsel-approved, effective-dated jurisdiction matrix.

## provenance, rights, and metadata standards

| standard | useful mapping | what it does not solve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [C2PA Content Credentials 2.4](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/specs/C2PA_Specification.html), april 2026, and [AI/ML guidance](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.4/ai-ml/ai_ml.html) | media binding, signed assertions, ingredients, actions, redaction declarations, time-stamps, credential revocation, validation status, AI disclosure | C2PA validates association, structure, signatures, and tamper evidence under its trust model. it does not determine truth, consent, lawful basis, facility authority, ownership, license validity, fair compensation, dataset membership, training influence, or model safety. capy records a C2PA validation result as one evidence reference only. |
| [W3C PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) and [PROV-DM](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/) | `entity`, `activity`, `agent`, `used`, `wasGeneratedBy`, `wasDerivedFrom`, `wasAssociatedWith`, invalidation, and delegation shape capy's lineage graph | PROV expresses claims about provenance; it neither authenticates those claims nor grants rights. capy adds digests, signatures, evidence, rights gates, and restrictions. |
| [W3C ODRL information model 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/odrl-model/) | permissions, prohibitions, duties, assignees, assets, constraints, and policy conflict concepts inform `use-policy` | ODRL can express a policy but does not show the assigner owns rights, the assignee consented, or the policy is lawful/enforceable. capy uses a narrower JSON contract and may export an ODRL view later. |
| [ISO/IEC TS 27560:2023 consent record information structure](https://www.iso.org/standard/80392.html) | consent record/receipt lifecycle, parties, purposes, processing, status, and proof inform `contributor-rights` | the full text is licensed, is marked for revision, and capy has not performed a conformance assessment. recording consent does not make consent valid. do not claim conformity without licensed review and testing. |
| [MLCommons Croissant 1.1](https://docs.mlcommons.org/croissant/docs/croissant-spec-1.1.html) and [Croissant RAI 1.0](https://docs.mlcommons.org/croissant/docs/croissant-rai-spec.html) | dataset metadata, resources, record sets, fields, distributions, PROV-O provenance, use information, lifecycle, limitations, and traceability export for accepted cohorts | Croissant improves dataset discoverability/interoperability; it does not prove rights, consent, redaction quality, or model lineage. never place restricted contributor data in public Croissant metadata. |
| [SPDX specification 3.0.1 AI profile](https://spdx.github.io/spdx-spec/v3.0.1/model/AI/AI/) | AI/model package inventory and declared/concluded licenses; optional export of model and dataset software-bill-of-materials data | a declared or concluded license is an assertion, not proof of authority or compatibility. personal-data and consent controls remain external. |
| [SPDX license list](https://spdx.org/licenses/) | canonical identifiers and expressions for accurately identified standard licenses | no identifier may be guessed. custom contributor/facility terms need their own URI and exact-text digest. |
| [JSON Schema draft 2020-12](https://json-schema.org/draft/2020-12/json-schema-core) | machine validation, identifiers, references, conditionals, and closed record shapes | shape validation cannot test legal sufficiency, signatures, authorization, chronology, referential integrity, base58 decoded lengths, or semantic lineage constraints. |
| [RFC 8785 JSON canonicalization scheme](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8785) | deterministic JSON bytes before hashing/signing | canonicalization does not authenticate a record. signatures, key governance, time, and trustworthy capture remain necessary. |

## risk, privacy, and incident frameworks

| framework | capy use | status/limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [NIST AI risk management framework 1.0](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) | govern, map, measure, and manage lifecycle risks; evaluation evidence and accountable decisions | voluntary; version 1.0 is under revision as of this review. not a certification or legal safe harbor. |
| [NIST privacy framework 1.0](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) | identify-p, govern-p, control-p, communicate-p, and protect-p outcomes inform minimization, rights, and retention | voluntary; NIST has an initial public draft for 1.1. capy maps to the current final 1.0, not the draft. |
| [NIST cybersecurity framework 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/publications/nist-cybersecurity-framework-csf-20) | govern, identify, protect, detect, respond, and recover structure the security controls and incident lifecycle | outcome framework, not prescriptive implementation or certification. |
| [NIST SP 800-61 rev. 3](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final) | incident preparation across risk management, detection, response, recovery, communication, and improvement | cybersecurity guidance does not determine privacy/safety notification duties; counsel owns the deadline matrix. |

## Solana and native USDC sources

| source | capy conclusion |
| --- | --- |
| [Solana transaction and token-balance JSON structures](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/json-structures), [`getTransaction`](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/http/gettransaction), and [`getSignaturesForAddress`](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/http/getsignaturesforaddress) | finalized ordinary transfers expose signatures, account participation, instructions, and token balance changes, and are address-searchable. |
| [Solana token-account creation](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics/create-token-account) | token accounts expose mint, owner, and amount; associated token accounts are deterministically related to owner and mint and are not a privacy layer. |
| [Solana Pay specification 1.1](https://solana.com/docs/tools/solana-pay/specification/version1-1) | `reference` is an account key intended for public lookup and memo content is recorded on-chain. capy defaults both off. |
| [Circle native USDC contract addresses](https://developers.circle.com/stablecoins/usdc-contract-addresses) and [Solana transfer quickstart](https://developers.circle.com/stablecoins/quickstart-transfer-10-usdc-on-solana) | native Solana USDC uses mint `EPjFWdd5AufqSSqeM2qN1xzybapC8G4wEGGkZwyTDt1v` and the classic token program in Circle's integration. its ordinary transfers do not gain Token-2022 confidential balances. |
| [Solana privacy options](https://solana.com/docs/finance/privacy) | Token-2022 confidential balances can hide amounts/balances while accounts and participation remain public; other beta or institution-operated options have distinct trust, custody, audit, and compliance boundaries. none is a drop-in privacy upgrade for current native USDC. |

an opaque off-chain payout reference reduces unnecessary joins inside capy. it does not change what Solana publishes. fresh wallets, separate transactions, omitted memos, private RPC transport, timing windows, and rotating treasury accounts only narrow specific correlation surfaces; none makes a public payout anonymous.

## conformance posture

capy may say its schemas are "informed by" or "mapped to" the sources above. capy must not claim legal compliance, certification, ISO conformity, C2PA proof of consent, W3C PROV truth, SPDX license validity, or NIST conformance without a separate scoped assessment and evidence.
